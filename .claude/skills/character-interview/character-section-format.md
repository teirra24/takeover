# Character Section Format

The locked output of one character interview. Built for this standalone skill; if the user has an original format from a larger story-bible skill, use theirs instead.

Keep it tight: the whole section should fit on one screen. Quoted lines are written to be copy-pasted straight into prompts.

---

## Template

```markdown
## [Character Name] — *[Role Tag]*

**Visual:** [One dense line: hair (color, length, cut, permanent features) · skin (tone + finish, never-drift) · face (jaw, cheekbones, chin, nose, lips) · eyes (shape, size, color, lift) · identity markers · height range, build, default stance · default expression.] **Never:** [locked never-clauses, comma separated].

**Backstory:** [One short paragraph or 3–4 tight bullets: origin, shaping events, what happened before the story starts.] *Formative detail:* [the one small, specific detail].

**Now:** [Where they are as the story begins — what they carry, what they're about to face, what they don't yet know.] *The gap:* [what they're doing vs. what they're actually feeling].

**Speech:** *"[register, texture, cadence, volume default, vocabulary tier, signature moves]"*

**Movement:** *"[gesture quality, body carriage, combat posture if relevant, habitual tics, gait]"*

**Stillness:** *"[at rest: weight, hands, expression, breath]"*

**Musical voice:** *"[range, singing timbre, signature register, style affinity, signature move — no artist or song names]"*   ← music scope only; omit otherwise

**[Sub-beat title]:** [Locked canon thread, written as observable behaviour.]   ← only if the user named one; omit otherwise
```

---

## Rules

- Name in the header only; the role tag is one to three words in italics.
- Visual is one line, compressed from the full interview. Order: hair, skin, face, eyes, markers, body, expression, then **Never:**.
- Speech, Movement, Stillness and Musical voice are each a single quoted, italic string of comma-separated descriptors, no full sentences.
- Backstory and Now stay brief. Their job is to keep later scenes consistent, not to tell the whole story.
- Sub-beats are bold-titled and only appear when the user locked a specific thread.
- No age numbers anywhere.

## Example

```markdown
## Seo-yun — *The Watcher*

**Visual:** cool ash-platinum blunt bob with permanent wispy temple pieces and a center part · cool fair skin, matte finish, never warm-shifted or tanned · sharp-sculpted jaw, high cheekbones, refined narrow nose, soft full lips · long almond eyes, dark brown, slight lift at the outer corner · three small silver studs climbing the left ear helix · tall, slim, upright, weight centered · neutral observing expression. **Never:** no beauty mark, no teeth-showing smile, no visible tattoos.

**Backstory:** Raised above her family's dry-cleaning shop; learned early to notice what people left in their pockets and say nothing. *Formative detail:* she still irons her own shirts at 2am when she can't sleep, because the steam is the only sound that quiets her head.

**Now:** Newly placed as the group's quiet strategist, she already suspects their manager is lying about the contract. *The gap:* she's projecting total calm while quietly planning an exit she hasn't told anyone about.

**Speech:** *"cool mid-register voice with clean smooth tone, measured volume, surgical sentence construction, long deliberate pauses, sophisticated vocabulary, never raises her voice in anger"*

**Movement:** *"minimal contained gestures, small precise head tilts, reactive counter-punch combat posture, prolonged steady looking, infrequent blinking"*

**Stillness:** *"deep settled stillness, square shoulders, one hand at the opposite elbow, neutral observing expression, slow breath"*
```
